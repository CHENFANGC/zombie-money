# 60-Second Demo Script

## 0:00 - 0:10

"This is Zombie Money. Instead of showing a user a table of vaults and APYs, it starts with a simple truth: your money is sleeping."

Show the home screen.
Pause on the sleeping balance, status pill, and breathing orb.

## 0:10 - 0:25

"The app detects idle stablecoins and turns that into one clear action: wake it up."

Tap `Wake it up`.

## 0:25 - 0:40

"Behind the scenes, the recommendation is grounded in real LI.FI Earn data. But instead of overwhelming the user with protocols and routes, Zombie Money chooses one calm, stablecoin-friendly option and explains why it fits."

Pause on the recommendation card, the why-fit reasons, and the earnings estimate card.

## 0:40 - 0:52

"For the hackathon sprint, the activation flow is staged for reliability, but the story stays believable."

Tap `Revive funds`.
Show the short activation progress sheet.

## 0:52 - 1:00

"Now the money is awake. Most wallets just store money. Zombie Money gives it a heartbeat."

Land on the success screen and hold on the active tags plus earnings card.
